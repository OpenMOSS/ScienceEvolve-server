# Review guidelines input for ScholarPeer G.8

Adapted summary of the [official ICLR 2025 reviewer guide](https://iclr.cc/Conferences/2025/ReviewerGuide), checked 2026-09-27. This is a local simulated review, not a conference submission.

Read the entire manuscript and relevant prior work. Identify the research problem, motivation and relationship to existing work. Evaluate technical correctness, experimental rigor, reproducibility, originality, clarity and the value of the knowledge contributed. Leading benchmark performance alone is neither necessary nor sufficient. Support judgements with specific evidence; distinguish demonstrated flaws from uncertainty and requests for clarification. Consider potential ethical issues and limitations.

Provide a contribution summary, comprehensive strengths and weaknesses, a reasoned recommendation, targeted questions that could change that recommendation, and separately identified suggestions for improvement. Remain constructive and willing to revise conclusions when new evidence arrives.

ScientistTwo requires an overall score from 1 to 10 and uses 8 as its stopping threshold. Preserve the ScholarPeer prompt's review format, expose confidence, and explain the score using the evidence. The threshold is a control parameter, not a target reviewers must award. Never substitute this review for independent experimental or integrity verification.
